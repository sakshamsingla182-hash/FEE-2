Welcome to Rosemary Public School

Rosemary Public School, located on Patiala Road in Sunam, Punjab, is a premier co-educational English medium institution affiliated with the CBSE. Founded in 2008 by NCERT Award Winner Sh. Kailash Chand Kansal, it offers comprehensive education from Nursery through Class XII across Science, Commerce, and Arts streams.Campus and AcademicsSet on an expansive 11,978-square-meter campus, the school features over 40 spacious classrooms and advanced digital facilities. Driven by its core motto, "Bal, Budh, Vinay, Vijay," the institution is dedicated to holistic child development. It focuses heavily on logic-based learning, moral integrity, and character-building. The total student enrollment is approximately 1,080, supported by an experienced faculty.Specialized Coaching and Facilities
